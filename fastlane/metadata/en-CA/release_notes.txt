This version makes AudioPlayLog clearer and keeps you company through December.

• Calmer list: Unplayed episodes stand out right away, played ones step back. Your progress sits in a slim line above the list.

• Up Next: At the top you now get a concrete suggestion for what to listen to next. Not in the mood? Tap "Another Suggestion".

• Add episodes by search: Type the number or part of the title, and AudioPlayLog finds the episode in your catalogs. Title, number and year are filled in for you.

• Christmas episodes: From December 1 to 26, Up Next gathers advent calendars and Christmas specials from your catalogs. Not your thing? Turn off "Seasonal touches" in Settings.

• Your listening year: Statistics now has your year in review with favorite series, strongest month and your top 3. Share it as an image.

Plus a clearer detail view, reorganized settings and more improvements under the hood.

Enjoy your listening!
