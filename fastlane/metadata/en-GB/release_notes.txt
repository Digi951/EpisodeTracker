This version gives you more control over your covers, plus a few small but noticeable improvements.

• Edit your cover: Rotate your episode cover, or crop it precisely with automatic edge detection. You'll find the new Edit button right next to your cover in the edit form.

• Personalization, front and center: A one-time hint on launch lets you know you can customize your app icon and accent color in Settings to match your taste.

• More reliable on iPad: The catalog progress count used to show one episode too many for collections with special episodes. That's fixed now.

There are also a few smaller improvements under the hood, including better language fallback for system languages AudioPlayLog doesn't directly support yet.

Enjoy your listening!
